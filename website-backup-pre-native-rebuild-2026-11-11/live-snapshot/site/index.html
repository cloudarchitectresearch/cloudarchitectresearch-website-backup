■ FAQ

Have Questions?

1. Do you support both new SAP implementations and existing environments?

Yes. We support implementation projects as well as existing SAP environments requiring configuration, process improvements, testing, stabilization, or ongoing functional support.

2. Which SAP areas do you support?

Our focus includes SAP S/4HANA, MM, Procure-to-Pay, EWM and warehouse processes, along with related business process and integration activities.

3. Can you help with testing, cutover and go-live?

Yes. We can support functional and integration testing, defect coordination, business readiness, cutover planning, go-live activities, hypercare, and post-go-live stabilization.

4. How does an engagement typically begin?

We start with an initial discussion to understand your current environment, business requirements, challenges, priorities, and the type of support you need.
